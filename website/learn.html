Tips

Learning Fingerspelling

✍️

Practice Daily

Start with your name, then move to common words. Try spelling everything you see for 5 minutes a day.

👀

Watch the Shape

Pay attention to hand shape, finger position, and palm orientation — each sign has a unique form.

🔤

Start Simple

Begin with letters that use distinct hand shapes (A, B, C, L, Y) before moving to similar ones (M, N, T).

🔄

Use Repetition

Type the same word multiple times until the sequence feels natural. Muscle memory builds with repetition.

🤟

Full ASL vs Fingerspelling

Fingerspelling spells words letter-by-letter. It's used for names, places, and when you don't know the sign.

📱

Practice Anywhere

Use this page on your phone too! Type words throughout the day to build vocabulary quickly.
