How the Model Was Trained

📥

Collect

17.5K ASL alphabet samples (A–Z, 0–9).

🔄

Augment

Mirror, rotate ±30°, scale & jitter — train-only.

⚖️

Balance

1,200 samples per class with class weights.

🧪

Train

3 DNNs, focal loss, cosine decay — 95%+ accuracy.

📊

Validate

Clean held-out sets, honest metrics.

🚀

Ship

Frozen EXE / DMG / AppImage on every release.

Why It Matters

Bridging Silence & Sound

Over 70 million deaf people worldwide use sign language as their primary language — yet most hearing people don't know it, creating barriers at schools, hospitals, banks and workplaces. AI Sign Bridge translates ASL fingerspelling into speech in real time, with no interpreter required.
