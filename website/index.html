How It Works

From Gesture to Speech in 4 Steps

The entire pipeline is designed for minimal latency while maintaining maximum accuracy.

1

Camera Capture

Webcam captures your hand sign at 30 FPS. OpenCV processes each frame for landmark detection.

2

Hand Landmarks

MediaPipe Tasks API detects 21 precise 3D hand landmarks — positions, angles, and distances.

3

AI Classification

A deep neural network ensemble (3 models, 500K+ parameters each) classifies the sign with 95%+ accuracy.

4

Speech Output

Offline TTS speaks the recognized sign aloud. Temporal smoothing prevents flickering between similar signs.

Why This Matters

Over 70 million deaf people worldwide use sign language as their primary means of communication. Yet most hearing people don't know sign language, creating barriers in everyday situations — at schools, hospitals, banks, and workplaces.

AI Sign Bridge was created to bridge this gap using cutting-edge AI. By translating American Sign Language fingerspelling into speech in real time, we enable natural conversation without requiring an interpreter.

This prototype was developed for an inter-school Innovation Challenge — a prototype building competition focused on advanced problem-solving, design thinking, and research-driven solutions. Participants presented their models with live demonstrations, supported by laptops and charts, with electricity provided at each station. The project aligns with the challenge themes of AI & IoT applications, sustainable technology, and social impact projects — addressing a real communication barrier faced by millions worldwide.

The system recognizes 37 distinct signs (A-Z, 0-9) with over 95% accuracy, running entirely offline on a standard laptop — exactly the kind of practical, scalable solution the competition sought to encourage.

The Road Ahead

Scaling the Future

From a prototype to a platform — exploring how AI Sign Bridge can grow to bridge communication gaps at scale.

01

Full Vocabulary Expansion

From 37 fingerspelling signs to 1,000+ ASL words, including dynamic signs (J, Z) and phrase-level translation using transformer-based sequence models for natural, flowing conversation.

02

Browser Deployment

Converting the model to ONNX format for in-browser inference. Zero install — just open a URL and start signing. Ideal for schools, hospitals, and public kiosks where installation isn't practical.

03

Mobile Application

Native iOS and Android apps with TensorFlow Lite or CoreML for on-device inference. Real-time sign language translation in your pocket, anywhere, anytime — no internet required.

04

Community Dataset

Open-source dataset contribution platform allowing users to record and contribute sign variations. Scales coverage across dialects, skin tones, lighting conditions, and signing styles.

05

Enterprise Deployment

Dedicated translation kiosks for hospitals, airports, banks, and schools. Multi-language sign support (ASL, ISL, BSL) with customizable vocabulary and accessibility compliance.

06

Real-Time Conversation

Bidirectional translation combining speech recognition for hearing users with sign-to-text/speech for deaf users. Natural, flowing conversation without barriers — the ultimate goal.
