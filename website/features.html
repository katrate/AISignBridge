Features

Everything AI Sign Bridge Can Do

A comprehensive breakdown of every feature powering the next generation of sign language translation.

Live Webcam Detection

Real-time video capture at 30 FPS using OpenCV. Frames are processed immediately through the MediaPipe hand landmark pipeline, detecting 21 precise 3D points on your hand. The system supports dynamic lighting conditions, different hand orientations, and works with both left and right hands.

30 FPSMediaPipe Tasks21 Landmarks640×480

Deep Neural Network Ensemble

Our classification engine uses an ensemble of 3 deep neural networks, each with 500K+ parameters. Features include residual MLP blocks, batch normalization, dropout regularization, and Swish activation. Focal loss training focuses on hard examples, while test-time augmentation (TTA) averages predictions across multiple noise-injected variants for maximum stability.

Residual BlocksFocal Loss3-Model EnsembleTTA

Intelligent Speech Output

Every recognized sign is spoken aloud using pyttsx3 offline TTS engine. The system intelligently queues words into natural phrases before speaking, preventing robotic one-word-at-a-time speech. A background worker thread handles speech without blocking the UI, and the engine supports voice selection, speed control, and volume adjustment.

pyttsx3Phrase QueueOffline TTSVoice Selection

Bidirectional Communication

Not just sign-to-speech — also speech-to-sign! Using Vosk offline speech recognition, you can speak a letter or number and see its ASL sign displayed visually. This creates a truly bidirectional communication bridge: deaf users sign and hear the translation; hearing users speak and see the sign.

Vosk STTBidirectionalSign DisplayOffline

100% Offline & Private

Every single component runs locally on your machine. No data ever leaves your computer. No cloud APIs, no internet required, no account needed. The model, the speech engine, the speech recognition — all of it runs on your hardware. Your privacy is guaranteed by design.

No CloudNo TrackingFully LocalPrivate

Optimized Real-Time Performance

Engineered for CPU inference with smart frame skipping (every-other-frame prediction), EMA temporal smoothing to prevent flickering, cooldown-based emission to avoid duplicate detections, and minimum hand presence detection. Runs at 15+ FPS on modern laptops without a GPU.

15+ FPS CPUEMA SmoothingCooldown SystemLow Latency

37 Recognized Signs

Supports all 26 letters (A-Z) and 10 digits (0-9), plus space. The model was trained on a balanced dataset of 45,000+ samples with aggressive data augmentation (rotation, scaling, jitter, mirroring) for robust real-world performance. Weak classes like O, C, and digits have dedicated upweighting in training.

A-Z0-945K SamplesBalanced

Retrainable Pipeline

The entire training pipeline is open and customizable. Collect your own data with the built-in data collector, augment it with rotation/scale/jitter, and retrain the model. Includes scripts for data collection, augmentation, feature extraction, and model training. New signs can be added by collecting just 100+ samples.
