How It Works

Browser-Based Hand Tracking

1
📷

Camera Activates

Your webcam streams video directly in the browser. All processing happens locally — no data sent anywhere.

2
✋

MediaPipe Tracks

Google's MediaPipe detects 21 hand landmarks in real time — tracking finger positions, angles, and depth.

3
🧠

Classifier Decides

A rule-based classifier analyzes finger extension states to identify the ASL sign from a limited set.

4
🔊

Result Displayed

The recognized letter appears on screen along with a confidence estimate. Works entirely in your browser.

# This demo runs 100% in your browser.
→ No data leaves your computer
→ No server required
→ Uses MediaPipe Tasks + WebGL
