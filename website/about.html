About

Our Mission

Making sign language accessible to everyone through the power of artificial intelligence.

Why AI Sign Bridge?

Over 70 million deaf people worldwide use sign language as their primary means of communication. Yet most hearing people don't know sign language, creating a significant communication barrier in everyday situations — at schools, hospitals, banks, and workplaces.

AI Sign Bridge was created to bridge this gap using cutting-edge artificial intelligence. By translating American Sign Language (ASL) fingerspelling into speech in real time, we enable natural conversation without requiring an interpreter. The system recognizes 37 distinct signs (A-Z, 0-9) with over 95% accuracy and speaks them aloud instantly.

Our approach is deliberately offline-first and privacy-preserving. All processing happens on your local machine — no data ever leaves your computer. This makes it suitable for sensitive environments like hospitals and schools.

Developed as part of an innovation challenge focused on AI & IoT applications for social impact, this prototype demonstrates how technology can break down communication barriers and create a more inclusive world.

Timeline

Our Journey

Phase 0 — Initial Build

Basic Prototype with 42 Features

Initial working prototype using MediaPipe + basic ML. 82% accuracy with severe class imbalance and train/test data leakage issues.

Phase 1 — Accuracy Fix

Feature Engineering + Ensemble

Fixed train/test leakage, added 132 features (geometric + normalized), implemented sklearn ensemble (HGB + MLP + SVM). Achieved 92.2% accuracy.

Phase 2 — Deep Learning

DNN Ensemble + Focal Loss + TTA

Replaced sklearn with deep residual MLPs. Focal loss training, on-the-fly augmentation, test-time averaging, 3-model ensemble. Target: 95-97% accuracy.

Phase 3 — Web Deployment (Planned)

Browser-Based Inference

Converting to ONNX for in-browser inference with MediaPipe web and ONNX Runtime Web. Fully client-side sign language translation in any browser.

The Road Ahead

Scaling the Future

How AI Sign Bridge can grow beyond a prototype to create lasting impact at scale.

01

Full Vocabulary Expansion

From 37 fingerspelling signs to 1,000+ ASL words, including dynamic signs (J, Z) and phrase-level translation using transformer-based sequence models for natural, flowing conversation.

02

Browser Deployment

Converting the model to ONNX format for in-browser inference. Zero install — just open a URL and start signing. Ideal for schools, hospitals, and public kiosks where installation isn't practical.

03

Mobile Application

Native iOS and Android apps with TensorFlow Lite or CoreML for on-device inference. Real-time sign language translation in your pocket, anywhere, anytime — no internet required.

04

Community Dataset

Open-source dataset contribution platform allowing users to record and contribute sign variations. Scales coverage across dialects, skin tones, lighting conditions, and signing styles.

05

Enterprise Deployment

Dedicated translation kiosks for hospitals, airports, banks, and schools. Multi-language sign support (ASL, ISL, BSL) with customizable vocabulary and accessibility compliance.

06

Real-Time Conversation

Bidirectional translation combining speech recognition for hearing users with sign-to-text/speech for deaf users. Natural, flowing conversation without barriers — the ultimate goal.
